# Code of Conduct

Tack is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, employers
and levels of experience. This code asks everyone who takes part to treat
one another with respect, and says what happens when someone does not. It
applies to everyone, maintainers included.

## Where it applies

In Tack's spaces: the repository on GitHub (issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and commit messages) and any channel or event run for Tack. It
also applies to anyone representing Tack elsewhere, such as presenting it at
a conference.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and considerate, and assume good faith.
- Welcome newcomers. A question that seems basic to you may be someone's
  first step into open source.
- Review code and ideas, not people: keep feedback on the technical merits.
- Be patient and clear. Many of us are not writing in our first language.
- Disagree without hostility. When consensus cannot be reached, accept the
  maintainers' decision.
- When you get something wrong, own it, apologize to those affected, and
  learn from it.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment of any kind, including intimidation, stalking and unwelcome
  attention.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about age, body size, disability,
  ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of
  experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
  appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or
  advances.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or events.

## Reporting a concern

Email **berk.geveci@kitware.com**. It helps to say what happened, where and
when (links or screenshots are best), and who was involved. You do not need
to be sure that something breaks this code to report it: it is better to
hear about it early. You will get a reply within a week.

Reports are kept confidential. Their details are shared only as needed to
resolve them, or where the law or someone's safety requires it.

## What happens next

The maintainers will look into the report, hear from the people involved
where appropriate, and respond in proportion. The response may be a private
explanation of why the behavior was not acceptable, a warning, or a
temporary or permanent ban from Tack's spaces. Serious or repeated
violations may lead straight to a ban.
